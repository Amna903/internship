# Retrieval before/after (Step 3)

> Generated by `python manage.py compare_retrieval`; do not edit by hand. Frozen Week 5 set (23 questions:
> 18 answerable, 5 unanswerable); embedder `ollama/nomic-embed-text:latest`. No language model is called.

**The corpus was deliberately built to be harder by the same author who builds the retrieval, so the size of any improvement may not transfer to other document sets.**

**Headline measure (chosen by the user after seeing the baseline, before any Step 3 work):** as-shipped hit
rate on answerable questions, with false retrieval on the 5 unanswerable questions as a guardrail
that must not rise (one miss = 20 points). Secondary: Recall@1 and MRR. Recall@3 was already at the ceiling.

## Result: the dev-chosen setting got worse on the primary measure

| run | setting | as shipped (primary) | false retrieval (guardrail) | R@1 | MRR | R@3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **before** (Week 4 retrieval) | semantic, chunk 80, floor 0.70, top-k 2 | **11 of 18 (61%)** | 0 of 5 (0%) | 83% | 0.97 | 100% |
| **after** (dev winner) | semantic, chunk 120, floor 0.74, top-k 2 | **5 of 18 (28%)** | 0 of 5 (0%) | 89% | 1.00 | 100% |

"After" is the configuration the pre-declared rules picked on the dev set (4800 configurations;
114 tied at the top with 4 of 12 dev hits). It was run on the frozen
set once and is reported as it came out; nothing was re-tuned on these results (rule 5).

**Why:** on the dev set, Week 4's retrieval returned something for 1 of the 3 unanswerable
questions (D13), so the rules required a configuration with no false retrievals on dev. The
dev winner's floor is 0.74 (Week 4: 0.70); on the frozen set,
6 correct answer(s) found by "before" are not returned by "after" (U03, U04, A02, A04, A06, A08). The real bottleneck is the abstention gate: correct-but-paraphrased
chunks and near-misses score in the same cosine range, and on the dev set the best hybrid configuration reached
4 of 12 hits against 4 for semantic. The secondary measures moved slightly (Recall@1 +1
question, MRR 0.97 → 1.00), from the larger chunk size.

**Shipping (champion/challenger, dev only): the champion stays.** It does not beat the champion on dev (a tie keeps the champion).
Dev: challenger 4 of 12 hits, 0 of 3 false;
champion 6 of 12 hits, 1 of 3 false.
Because no challenger ships, the safety gate (rule 7) did not run.
