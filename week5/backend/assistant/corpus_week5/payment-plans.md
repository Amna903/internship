# Payment plans (fictional clinic)

A balance of more than 120 euros can be split into up to 3 monthly payments. Payment plans are arranged by
the billing team and can be requested in the patient app. No interest or extra fee is charged for a payment
plan. If a monthly payment is missed, the plan ends and the remaining balance is due within 30 days. Refunds
for payments made under a plan follow the normal billing and refund policy.
