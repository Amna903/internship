# Sending records to another provider (fictional clinic)

The clinic can send a copy of a patient's records to another healthcare provider at the patient's request.
The request must be signed by the patient, or by someone the patient has authorised in writing. Records are
sent within 15 working days of a complete request. The first copy sent to another provider is free; each
additional paper copy costs 10 euros. Requests are made through the patient app or at the front desk.
