# Interpreter services (fictional clinic)

The clinic provides a professional interpreter free of charge for any appointment. An interpreter should be
requested when booking, or at least 3 working days before the appointment. For telehealth visits, the
interpreter joins the video call or takes part by phone. Family members may help with booking, but the
clinic offers a professional interpreter for the visit itself. Interpreter requests can be made in the patient
app or by calling the clinic's main line.
