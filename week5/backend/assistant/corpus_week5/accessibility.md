# Accessibility (fictional clinic)

The clinic has a step-free entrance and a lift to every floor. A hearing loop is available at the front desk
and in every consultation room. Patients who need a longer appointment slot because of a disability can ask
for one when booking, at no extra cost. Assistance animals are welcome in all areas of the clinic. Requests for
adjustments can be made in the patient app or by phone at least 48 hours before the appointment.
