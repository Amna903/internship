# Parking and arrival (fictional clinic)

Patients can park free of charge for up to 3 hours in the car park behind the clinic. Parking tickets are
validated at the front desk; an unvalidated ticket is charged at the car park's hourly rate. Two accessible
parking spaces are kept next to the main entrance. Bicycle racks are available beside the side door. Patients
should arrive 10 minutes before an in-person appointment to check in at the front desk.
