# Cancellation policy (fictional clinic)

Appointments can be cancelled free of charge up to 24 hours before the start time. Cancellations with less
than 24 hours' notice are recorded as late cancellations. A late cancellation fee of 15 euros applies to the
third late cancellation within 12 months, and to every late cancellation after that. Missed appointments
without any cancellation are recorded as no-shows and are treated the same way as late cancellations.
Cancellations can be made in the patient app, on the website, or by calling the clinic's main line.
