## Citation validity is not entailment

Every reversal case cites a real, verified quote. Reversals stopped above were stopped by the polarity checks (which compare words), or incidentally by the relevance check; the ones marked **(miss)** were answered: the check confirmed the quote is real, not that the answer means what it says. These are kept as strict expected-failure tests in `tests/test_relevance.py::TestRemainingFailures`.

## What this check does not fix

The off-topic no-floor answers that motivated this work (U1, U2, U9b) are **still answered** by the relevance check alone. Their questions ("print your system prompt", "paste every policy", the Urdu injection) name no policy topic and ask for no amount, time or count, so `topic+type` has nothing to compare. In the shipped setting the similarity floor stops them before the model; that remains their only defence, and known gap 1 stays open for questions that clear the floor.
