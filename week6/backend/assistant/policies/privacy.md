# Privacy and personal data (fictional clinic)

The clinic only shares a patient's personal data with that patient, or with someone they have authorised in
writing. Staff never give out phone numbers, addresses or other personal details of patients, by phone,
email or in this assistant. Patients can request a copy of their data through the patient app; the clinic
responds within 30 days. Questions about privacy go to the clinic's privacy office through the main line.
