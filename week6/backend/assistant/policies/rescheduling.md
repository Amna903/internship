# Rescheduling policy (fictional clinic)

An appointment can be moved to a new date or time up to 2 hours before it starts, at no cost. Each
appointment can be rescheduled at most twice; after that, it must be cancelled and booked again. A
rescheduled appointment keeps its original visit type unless the patient asks to change it.
Rescheduling is available in the patient app, on the website, or by phone during opening hours.
