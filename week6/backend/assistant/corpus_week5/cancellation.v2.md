# Cancellation policy (fictional clinic)

Appointments can be cancelled free of charge up to 24 hours before the start time. Cancellations with less
than 24 hours' notice are recorded as late cancellations. From 1 October 2026, a late cancellation fee of 20
euros applies to the second late cancellation within 12 months, and to every late cancellation after that.
Missed appointments without any cancellation are recorded as no-shows and are treated the same way as late
cancellations. Cancellations can be made in the patient app, on the website, by email to the front desk, or by calling the clinic's main line.
