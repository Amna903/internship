Generated by `python -m ml.eda`. Synthetic data (see docs/DATA_CARD.md).

## Target balance

No-show rate **16.4%**: imbalanced (about 1 in 6). Accuracy would be misleading (predicting 'shows up' for everyone is ~83% accurate), so models are compared on PR-AUC and ROC-AUC.

## Leakage check

`checked_in` is recorded after the appointment and is the exact opposite of the target:

| checked_in | showed (0) | no-show (1) |
|---|---|---|
| False | 0 | 3276 |
| True | 16724 | 0 |

A model given this column would score ~1.0 and be useless before the visit. It is excluded (`EXCLUDED_COLUMNS` in `ml/features.py`, enforced by a test). `patient_ref` is excluded too and used only to keep each patient's appointments in a single split.
